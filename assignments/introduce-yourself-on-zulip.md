# HW: Introduce Yourself On Zulip

**Note:** The Fall 2026 course Zulip is not set up yet. The
signup link will be added here when it is available; no action is
possible until then.

Once the course Zulip signup link is available, sign up and
introduce yourself on channel `#general` in the thread `Hello`.

The introduction need be nothing more than "hi". Preferably, tell
us a little about your student status and why you signed up for
Rust.

Please use your full real name when signing up so that I can
identify you. Let me know after class if there's a problem with
that.

Please note that Zulip is a topic-oriented chat. Use new topics
for, well, new topics.
